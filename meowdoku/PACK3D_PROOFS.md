Every level of `pack3d.json` is checked three ways (generated by `make_pack3d_proofs.py`):

1. **Z3** enumerates all solutions of the level's constraints (one cat per layer in each direction, one per colour, no touching); it must find exactly one, and it must be the stored solution.
2. **Backtracking** in `nd.py` (independent code) counted the solutions when the level was generated.
3. **A derivation**: for 3×3×3 levels, the complete list of the 8 possible three-cat placements with their colours; for larger levels, the chain of direct rules (single candidate, common attack, colours locked in slices, slices made of few colours) that forces every cat.  Each rule is proven sound in `RULES.md` / `lean/`, and the argument only uses separating maps and touching, so it holds unchanged in 3D.  A derivation that reaches all n cats *is* a uniqueness proof: any solution must agree with every forced step.

Cells are written L‹layer›r‹row›c‹column›, counted from 1.
